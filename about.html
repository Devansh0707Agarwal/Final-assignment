Our story

Started in a home kitchen, still baked in one.

The Weekend Oven is run by Vandita Agarwal from her home kitchen in Ulubari, Guwahati. What began as cakes for family birthdays slowly turned into something friends, neighbours, and their friends started asking for too. There's no shopfront and no staff — just one kitchen, careful ingredients, and cakes made to order rather than kept on a shelf. Every order is delivered directly, so there's no shop to visit — just message ahead.

What we care about

Made to order

Nothing is pre-made or frozen. Every cake is baked after we've spoken to you about the occasion.

Real ingredients

Good butter, real chocolate, fresh fruit where possible — nothing artificial that we can avoid.

Small batches

We take on a limited number of orders each week so every cake gets proper attention.
